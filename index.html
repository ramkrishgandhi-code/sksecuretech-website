How SK Tailoring Works

From customer to completed delivery — one clear flow.

A simple 10-step workflow designed around real tailoring-shop work.

1Choose CustomerSearch existing customer or create a new profile.
2Choose GarmentSelect Men, Women, Girls or Boys garment.
3Select StyleChoose style and design options.
4Enter MeasurementsGarment-specific measurements with visual guidance.
5Add ReferencesPhotos, special notes and voice instructions.
6Charges & DeliverySet price, advance and promised delivery.
7Assign WorkerAllocate the tailoring job to a worker.
8Track ProgressFollow work queue and production status.
9Record PaymentTrack total, advance and remaining balance.
10Ready / Delivery / CompleteFinish the order and delivery workflow.
